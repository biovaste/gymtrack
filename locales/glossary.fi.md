# Finnish gym language

Use concise, natural Finnish. Address the user directly. Keep GymTrack unchanged.
Never translate stored identifiers or data values; translate their display labels.
Each key has its own context and approval, even when words match.

Translate complete messages, preserving named placeholders exactly. Avoid literal
English word order. Exercise names should be familiar Finnish gym names; retain
recognized terms when no natural Finnish equivalent exists. Preserve units such
as kg and abbreviations such as RPE. Initial machine drafts are immediately usable
in the app; human edits improve them and approval records review rather than access.
